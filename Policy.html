Policies

ID Description
P1 If an Asset is Confidential, it must be stored in a Secure SharePoint site (or equivalent secure recordkeeping location).
P2 A Document that is Confidential or higher must carry a watermark/visible indicator on every page.
P3 External emails must not include Restricted or Confidential attachments.
P4 When an asset is processed on a GenAI platform, the asset’s classification must not exceed the platform’s maximum allowed class.
P5 An asset’s retention must follow the retention period for its classification.
P6 If an asset has a “Confidential” label, its classification must be ≥ Confidential.
P7 .zip or .pst files that are Internal or higher must not be sent to GenAI platforms.
P8 Confidential physical records must be stored in a Secure cabinet and be logged.
P9 Documents with class ≥ Internal must have at least an Internal label (or higher).
P10 Assets with class ≥ Internal must have a named owner and an org-unit group owner.
P11 High-severity alerts must be assigned within the required time; unassigned high alerts are non-compliant.
P12 Assets must be stored only in services approved for their org unit.
P13 A GenAI job must run only in an approved tenant/region; platforms limited to Internal must not process Confidential.
P14 Per-unit restrictions may ban external email for Restricted (or higher).
P15 Per-unit rules may require watermarks for ≥ Internal documents.
P16 Per-unit rules may require a minimum label level for assets.
P17 Restricted data must not be moved via USB.
P18 Internal data may be sent via Email (positive allowance).
P19 Only a Records Officer may change an asset’s classification.
P20 A DLP scanner may automatically apply the Confidential label; provenance must show it was applied by DLP.
P21 Assets must use storage services approved by their unit.
P22 GenAI usage must be in tenants/platforms approved for the unit.
P23 External email may be banned by unit for certain classifications.
P24 Watermarks may be required by unit for certain classifications.
P25 Label minimum may be set by unit for certain classifications.
P26 Retention may be set by unit per classification.
P27 Who may change classification may be limited by unit.
P28 GenAI tenant control may be limited by unit (approved tenants only).
P29 Only a Site Owner may move a Confidential asset into a Secure site.
P30 Only approved AU tenants may process data; a platform with Internal limit must not process Confidential.
P31 Restricted data cannot use USB.
P32 Internal data may use Email.
P33 Internal is allowed via Email but not via USB.
P34 Storage services must resolve Confidential items to a Secure site (service-side rule).
P35 Page labelling (header/footer/watermark) is required for Internal/Sensitive/Confidential documents.
P36 Emails for Internal+ require a subject label token; higher classes may require sensitivity property set.
P37 Assets of Internal+ must have a visible label when viewed and a file-property label recorded.
P38 When using Bing/Copilot, the user must be signed in to the organisation account before uploading Internal+.
P39 Azure OpenAI is allowed for Public/Internal only.
P40 Other public GenAI (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini) is Public-only.
P41 Confidential in Teams chat is allowed only if the chat is linked to a Secure site or the content is sanitised and membership is clean.
P42 OneDrive is not for Confidential; Internal/Sensitive allowed only if draft/WIP and labelled.
P43 Content Manager/TRIM is appropriate for all classes (allow list).
P44 Google Workspace/Drive is not authorised; Google Drive allowed only for legacy content (no new content).
P45 Network Drive/eResearch may store Public/Internal only.
P46 For Internal+ sent externally: encrypt, send password out-of-band, prefer links; for Confidential also disable download/print.
P47 For Internal+ on portable media: encrypt, hand-deliver, and log custody.
P48 Archiving and destruction methods must be appropriate to classification (secure routes and destruction).
